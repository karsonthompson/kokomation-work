The new popup

Step 1 of 4: pick a card, every card wins 10% off. Then email, an optional text step, and the discount applies on its own.

Open the full popup

What it improves

  • Pick-a-card up front. Every card hides 10% off, so the offer is the first thing visitors touch. The email field only appears after they've earned the discount.
  • Split panel, photo on its own side. Copy sits on a solid panel in your own colors and fonts, and the photo fills its own column. No text sits on top of the photo.
  • Four steps, and only email is required. Pick a card, email, an optional text step that can be skipped, then the discount applies on its own. No code to copy.

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
