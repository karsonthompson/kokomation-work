Aluminum, Explained
Why the Dings Are the Point
Since 1938, Zero Halliburton has built aluminum travel cases for strength and protection. Aluminum will show dings and scratches, possibly even after the first use. The brand calls them recognizable badges of travel, and they do not expose what is inside to damage. To clean a case, wipe it gently with a soft, damp cloth. To bring out the natural shine, buff with a mild, silica free polishing wax.

Double Ribbed for Strength
In 1946 the brand introduced its iconic double ribbed design in high strength anodized aluminum to strengthen the case. It became the trademark you still see on every case today, from carry on luggage to the attache case.
