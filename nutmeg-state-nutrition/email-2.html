What Is the Proti Diet?
It is a high-protein, low-calorie plan built around portion-controlled shakes, bars, soups, drinks and snacks. The popular 5-2-1 day is five Proti Foods, two protein bars, and one meal of lean protein with two servings of vegetables.
The plan aims for about 80 to 120 grams of protein a day. Eating enough protein helps you stay full between meals and protect lean muscle while you lose, so more of the weight lost comes from fat. Talk with your doctor before starting a low-calorie plan.
