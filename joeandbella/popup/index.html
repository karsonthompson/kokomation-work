What it improves

  • Nothing before the ask. The first thing a visitor meets today is a form. The rebuild's first screen is the offer itself, one tap to claim, and the email field comes second.
  • Email only. The current form never asks for a phone number. The rebuild adds an optional text step, skippable, and the 15% still activates either way, with no code to copy.
  • Nothing speaks to your buyer. Today's popup could belong to any clothing brand. The rebuild is written for yours: dressing made easy, magnetic closures and side zips, right on the first screen.

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
