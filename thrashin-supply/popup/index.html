I redesigned Thrashin Supply's popup form.

The current popup

Today the popup asks for an email address in exchange for updates on new drops. It offers no discount and no other incentive to sign up.

The current popup: an email field traded for new drops updates, with no discount offered.

The new popup

Step 1 of 4: the claim. 15% off, stated up front, one tap to start.

Open the full popup

What it improves

  • The current popup asks visitors to subscribe for new-drop updates without an immediate incentive. This concept gives them a clear reason to join now.
  • The proposed 15% offer appears before the form, so shoppers see the value before entering an email.
  • An optional text step can capture additional subscribers without making a phone number mandatory.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
