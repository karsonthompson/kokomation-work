Choosing knobs and pulls: four lessons
Material Matters
brass, bronze, steel, nickel
Choose high quality metals like brass, stainless steel, or gold plated finishes that stand up to daily use while retaining their beauty. Our knobs and pulls are crafted from solid brass, bronze, stainless steel, pewter and nickel.
Finish With Purpose
match, or tastefully contrast
Match or tastefully contrast hardware finishes with your fixtures, appliances, and cabinetry to create a cohesive look across the room.
Size and Scale
balance the cabinetry
Consider the size of your cabinets and drawers when selecting knobs and pulls. Larger spaces often benefit from more substantial hardware for visual balance.
Consistency or Statement
one room, one decision
For a seamless look, choose matching hardware throughout a room. For a more dynamic effect, mix complementary styles or reserve statement pieces for focal points like kitchen islands or built in bars.
