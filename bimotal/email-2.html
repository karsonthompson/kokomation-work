Standard Or MAX?
At 750W, Elevate Standard has more than enough power for most trail riding. The quick release motor pops on for full ebike power and off again in seconds when you want the bike analog, with no permanent mods, and it can be shared across bikes.
 
Elevate MAX adds 500W, for 1250W total, for faster acceleration and better performance on steep inclines. Top speed and torque do not change, and increased power may affect range. MAX exceeds the US legal limit for ebike power and is intended for private land and OHV trails only.
