Goal sizes by age, in short
U.S. Soccer sets goal sizes by format. Players move from 4v4 to 7v7 to 9v9 before full size 11v11 at U13, and for the smaller formats a goal under the maximum still meets the standard.
So one well chosen goal can cover more than one age group: up to 6x4 ft for U5 to U8, 12x6.5 ft recommended for U9 to U10, and 18.5x6.5 ft recommended for U11 to U12.
Full size is 24x8 ft, the goal used for 11v11 from U13 upward.
