How to Use the Powder
Add one scoop (1 TBSP) directly to the drum before the laundry, in standard and HE machines alike, and wash in warm or hot water. For cold water loads, pre-dissolve the powder in hot water first, or reach for All Sport instead.
 
Hard water at home? Add half a cup of white vinegar to balance the pH; vinegar is a natural fabric softener. For extra dirty or stained loads, boost the wash with Oxygen Whitener, a chlorine-free, color-safe bleach alternative.
