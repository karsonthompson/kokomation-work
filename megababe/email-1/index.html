What it improves

  • The offer is the headline. 15% off and the code sit in a bar at the very top, and the hero leads with the same offer and a shop button, so the deal is clear before a single product appears.
  • The lineup is the hero. The top image shows the No Rub Club products together, so a new visitor sees the chafe range at a glance, not one accessory shot.
