What it improves

  • No popup today. The site has no signup popup, so the traffic it already gets leaves without ever joining the email list.
  • 10% off, stated up front. The verified first-order offer headlines step one, paired with a pick: retro classics, modern games, consoles and gear, or cards and collectibles.
  • Email first, phone optional. The email step needs no phone number, the text step is an optional extra, and the code lands by email.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
