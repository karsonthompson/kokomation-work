What it improves

  • Hidden behind another popup. Shoppers have to clear a location selector before the signup ever gets a chance to show.
  • Too much asked at once. Email and phone number are both demanded on a single step, before the shopper has any reason to hand over either.
  • Split into steps. The rebuild leads with the 10% off, takes email first, and makes the text step optional. Skipping it still activates the discount.

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
