From the Beginner’s Guide to Off-Grid Solar Power
Four parts. Batteries first.
Every off grid solar system has four key components: panels convert sunlight to DC, batteries store that energy, a charge controller charges the battery properly, and an inverter converts DC to household AC.
The guide’s counterintuitive lesson: your batteries are the most important component, and your loads decide the size and cost of everything else. Heat and cooling with wood or propane, and the system you need gets much smaller.
