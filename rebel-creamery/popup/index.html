I redesigned Rebel Creamery's popup form.

The current popup

Rebel Creamery has no signup popup. The only ways to join the list are an inline form that asks for a ZIP code and offers nothing in return, and a footer signup box whose submit button triggers a CAPTCHA.

The current signup: no popup, a ZIP code demanded up front, and a CAPTCHA on the footer box.

The new popup

Step 1 of 4: the claim. 15% off, stated up front, one tap to start.

Open the full popup

What it improves

  • No popup at all today. The only captures on the site are an inline form demanding a ZIP code for no incentive and a footer box whose submit triggers a CAPTCHA.
  • 15% off leads. The offer sits on the first screen, paired with a flavor style pick (pints, stick bars, or both), so step one gives a reason before it asks for anything.
  • Email only, no ZIP. The email step asks for just an email, the text step stays optional, and the code arrives by email.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
