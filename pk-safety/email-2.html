Two checks,
two different jobs
Bump testing verifies that a gas monitor will detect its target gas, and it should happen before each use. If a monitor fails its bump test, a full calibration is required. Calibration adjusts the instrument readings to a known concentration of test gas, so readings and alarms stay accurate. When in doubt, PK Safety points you to the owner manual and the manufacturer guidelines for your instrument.
