What it improves

  • It asks for everything and offers nothing. First and last name, email, ZIP code, nine interest checkboxes, and an acreage question, all to join a newsletter. The rebuild starts with one email field.
  • The offer leads. 15% off the first order, stated on the first screen, instead of no incentive at all.
  • Email and text, not just email. The rebuild adds an optional text step, and the 15% auto applies, so there is no code to copy or lose. The profiling questions can come later, after the signup.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
