Cut

GIA grades color from D, colorless, to Z, light yellow. Near colorless G to J often gives better value, since the top grades carry a premium out of proportion to how much whiter they look.

Carat, and the fifth check
Carat is weight, not size: one carat equals 0.2 grams and 100 points. A 0.90 carat stone sits just below the one carat premium and looks nearly identical, so the savings can buy a higher cut grade.
