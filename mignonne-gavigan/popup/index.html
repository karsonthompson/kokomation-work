What I changed

  • No popup today. Mignonne Gavigan has no signup popup, so the traffic the site already gets leaves without ever joining the email list.
  • Scratch to reveal 15% off. The scratch step reveals “You've Got 15% Off” before the email ask, and the offer is 15% off the first order.
  • Email claims it. The email step locks the 15% in, and first looks by text are optional.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one signs up 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that 2 point gap is 200 extra people in the welcome flow every month, hearing the offer while the visit is still fresh.
