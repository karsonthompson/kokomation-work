Why walnut works
Walnut is a dark brown hardwood with natural variation in colour and grain, from deep brown to blonde to reddish brown. It is hard, it holds up well, and no two pieces are exactly alike. When Loewen says walnut, it means the wood itself, never a walnut coloured stain on another species.
 
The finish decides durability and sheen, and Loewen finishes tables three ways: Rubio, Osmo and Conversion Varnish. Every design can be adjusted in size, shape, wood, finish, base and edge until it is just right for your space.
