Caring for teak, the Harbour way
Natural teak weathers to a soft silver grey over time, and that patina is part of its beauty. The wood expands and contracts with the climate, and its natural oils can rise to the surface after rain. None of that affects the strength of the frame.
Keep the original finish with Harbour Teak Cleaner at the beginning and end of each season. Between cleans, hose the piece down and scrub in the direction of the grain.
Near salt water, rinse weekly to remove salt build up, and use a custom fit cover when the piece is not in use.
