What it improves

  • The offer is the headline. 15% off and the code sit in a bar at the very top, above the imagery, and the same code comes back at the close. The discount is never left to hunt for.
  • Four benefits, each with its own visual. Organic Soils, Blended Fertilizers, Enriching Mulches and OMRI Listed get their own circle instead of a paragraph of text, all in the brand’s own colors and type.
  • One clear path to the first order. Offer first, then the brand, then the products, then the offer again at the end. Every button links to a real page on coastofmaine.com, and the footer points at the main collections.
