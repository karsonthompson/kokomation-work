The new popup

Step 1 of 4: 10% off on the first screen, before any questions.

Open the full popup

What it improves

  • The offer leads, not the quiz. The current popup runs a quiz before the email capture, so a visitor has to answer questions before the signup. This flow puts the claim first: 10% off stated on screen one, then one email step.
  • The differentiator does the persuading. The claim screen carries the strongest fact on the site: the only quilted weighted blanket made with 100% cotton, no synthetics and no polyester. That is a reason to hand over an email address.
  • The inbox keeps the promise. The SMS step is optional and skipping it still activates the 10%, and Email 1 prints the real code, WELCOME10, so what the popup promises is exactly what a subscriber gets.
