What it improves

  • The offer stays in view. The 5% off from the first email returns in a reminder bar at the top and again in a code box at the close, so nobody has to go back and hunt for it.
  • The best sellers do the teaching. Real products, real prices and real photos, each one linked to its own product page, with the story behind the range above them.
  • Education first, one clear path to shop. The first email delivers the offer; this one earns the trust, then points at one next step. Every button links to a real page on tilemart.com, and the footer points at the main collections.
