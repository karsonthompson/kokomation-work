What I changed

  • No popup today. CleanAirKits has no signup popup, so the traffic the site already gets leaves without ever joining the email list.
  • A welcome perk, matched to the home. A purifier-match pick opens the flow, and the welcome perk locks in at the email step.
  • Email, then optional text. The email step claims the perk, and clean-air alerts by text are optional.

The average popup signs up 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one signs up 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that 2 point gap is 200 extra people in the welcome flow every month, hearing the offer while the visit is still fresh.
