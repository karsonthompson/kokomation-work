Our Story
Farming that gives back
In the 1990s the founders saw the damage chemical pesticides, fertilizers and engineered seeds were doing to farms in India. They built Organic India around Tulsi, also known as Holy Basil, and around the farmers who grow it.
That model now supports 3,000 farm partnerships, along with community health care and education. Organic India is a certified B Corp, meeting high standards of social and environmental performance and transparency.
