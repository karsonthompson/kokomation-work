Joe Invented It.
Gratz Makes It.
Gratz began in 1929 as a New York metalworking shop founded by MIT trained engineer Frank Gratz. In the late 1960s, his son Donald began collaborating with Romana Kryzanowska, Joseph Pilates's protege, to fabricate apparatus true to the original designs. That collaboration made Gratz the first manufacturer of classical Pilates apparatus.
The Four Values Behind Every Piece
Authenticity: the original manufacturer Craftsmanship: built by skilled artisans Community: teachers and students worldwide
Made In Philadelphia
After more than 80 years in New York, production moved to Philadelphia in 2014. Every Reformer, Cadillac, Chair, Barrel and Mat is made there with high quality materials, built to give the feel, resistance and durability the classical method requires.
