What I’d test

What I’d measure

I’d compare popup views, email signups, signup rate and revenue per visitor. For illustration, if 10,000 visitors see the form and the signup rate rises from 3% to 5%, that’s 200 additional subscribers. Actual results depend on the audience, offer and test.
