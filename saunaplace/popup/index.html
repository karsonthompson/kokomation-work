What it improves

  • One step for a huge purchase. Almost nobody buys a sauna on the first visit, and this single email box is the only way the store can follow up.
  • Nothing for the undecided. A shopper who is still deciding gets no help and no reason to come back, and the store gets no way to reach them.
  • Built for a long decision. The rebuild leads with its offer, takes email first, and adds an optional text step, so the store can stay in touch while the shopper decides.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
