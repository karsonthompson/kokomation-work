How a piece comes to be
Fairmined Gold
Impact at the source
Our first priority is materials that drive social impact at the source: Fairmined gold, and gemstones from mining communities we know and support.
Recycled and Antique
What already exists
When new materials cannot provide the consistency or scale a collection requires, we turn to recycled metals, antique diamonds, and heritage stones from previous generations.
Made in Brooklyn
Our own studio
All WWAKE pieces are made in our Brooklyn studio and New York diamond district. We built the studio in 2015 as a clean, low toxic environment for our jewelers.
Wing Yau
Founder, started 2012
Wing grew up between Papua New Guinea, Peru, and the Pacific Northwest, and studied sculpture at RISD. She began making necklaces rooted in textile sculpture, work that led to WWAKE in 2012.
Jewelry as an archive
A stone formed over millions of years. A miner, a cutter, a setter. Gold does not forget: it carries its story forward, and so does the piece you choose.
Read Our Ethos
WWAKE contributes a minimum of 1% of annual sales to organizations working on immigrant advocacy, housing security, and support for marginalized communities.
