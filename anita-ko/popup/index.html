What I changed

  • No popup today. Anita Ko has no signup popup, so the traffic the site already gets leaves without ever joining the email list.
  • An inner-circle offer, not a generic signup. A style pick from The Diamond Edit opens the flow, and the promise is private previews and early access.
  • Email unlocks the access. The email step opens the inner circle, and first looks by text are optional.

The average popup signs up 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one signs up 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that 2 point gap is 200 extra people in the welcome flow every month, hearing the offer while the visit is still fresh.
