I redesigned Cousins Maine Lobster's popup form.

The current popup

There is no popup on the site today. The only way to join the email list is a mailing list box in the footer, and it gives no discount and no other reason to sign up.

The current setup: no popup at all, just a footer mailing list box.

The new popup

Step 1 of 4: pick a card. 15% off stated up front, and every card wins it.

Open the full popup

What it improves

  • No popup at all. The only signup on the store is a footer box that asks for an email address and offers nothing in return.
  • The offer leads. 15% off is the headline of the first screen, so visitors know what signing up earns before they pick a card.
  • Email first, text optional. One email claims the 15%. The text step can be skipped, and the discount applies on its own, with no code to copy.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
