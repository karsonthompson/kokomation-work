What it improves

  • The offer stays in view. The 15% off from the first email returns in a reminder bar at the top and again in a code box at the close, so nobody has to go back and hunt for it.
  • The brand’s values carry the teaching. One card with icons lays out how the products are made and why, in the brand’s own words. Nothing is claimed that the site does not say.
  • Education first, one clear path to shop. The first email delivers the offer; this one earns the trust, then points at one next step. Every button links to a real page on jamestownmattress.com, and the footer points at the main collections.
