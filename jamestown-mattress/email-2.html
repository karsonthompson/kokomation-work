A Family Tradition
Since 1886, in Jamestown, New York
The company began as the W. I. Blystone Mattress Company in 1886. In 1981, the Pullan family took the reins with a modest team of three artisans. Today, 40 dedicated employees and eight stores across Western New York and northwestern Pennsylvania keep the work local, with multiple generations of the family in the day-to-day operations.
