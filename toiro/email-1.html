WHY A CLAY POT

One pot, centuries in the making.

A donabe is a Japanese clay pot built for one-pot cooking over open flame. Ours are Iga-yaki, shaped and finished by hand at Nagatani-en in Iga, Japan, from porous clay formed in the ancient bed of Lake Biwa.

That clay is the whole story. It heats slowly, holds its heat long after the flame is off, and cooks food gently and evenly, from the core out.

“Every donabe we send out is one I would cook with in my own kitchen. Start with one pot. It will change how you cook.”
