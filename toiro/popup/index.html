The current popup

There is no popup on the site today. The only way to join the email list is a subscribe box in the footer, and it offers no discount and no other reason to sign up.

The current setup: no popup at all, just a footer mailing list box with no incentive.

The new popup

Step 1 of 4: the claim. 15% off, stated up front, one tap to start.

Open the full popup

What it improves

  • Right now the email signup is tucked into the footer. A popup brings the invitation to visitors who may never scroll that far.
  • The proposed 15% welcome offer gives first-time shoppers a concrete reason to subscribe instead of a generic newsletter request.
  • Email is the only required contact step; SMS remains optional, keeping the path to signup simple.

Why the signup rate matters

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
