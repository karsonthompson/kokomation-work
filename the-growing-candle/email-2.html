Started in a basement
Chris and Cyndi started Hyggelight in their basement with one belief: beautiful things should be made with purpose, warmth and less everyday waste.
Each soy candle is hand poured into a ceramic container with a plantable seed label, and every label holds a wildflower blend of 10 varieties, from Snapdragon to Lemon Mint. Hyggelight is a Certified B Corp.
