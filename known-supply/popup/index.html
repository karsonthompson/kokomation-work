What it improves

  • Nothing offered in return. The current flyout asks for an email address in exchange for notifications. The rebuild leads with 15% off the first order.
  • Broken on the screen that matters. On a phone, the current headline runs underneath the photo. In the rebuild the words sit on their own panel and are never covered.
  • Email and text, not just email. The rebuild adds an optional text step, and the 15% auto applies, so there is no code to copy or lose.

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
