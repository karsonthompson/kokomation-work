What it improves

  • No product in the design. The popup is plain text on a blank panel: a headline, two fields and a button, with none of the leather the brand is known for.
  • A birthday for a discount. The phone form asks for an email address and a birthday on one step to unlock 15% off a first order.
  • Simpler steps. The rebuild leads with the same 15% off, takes email first, and drops the birthday from the signup. The text step stays optional.

The average popup signs up about 2 to 3 of every 100 visitors. A strong one gets 5 or more. On 10,000 visitors a month, that gap is 200 extra people in your welcome flow every single month, from traffic you already paid for. The welcome flow is where first orders come from, so those 200 people are the difference between email being a side channel and email being a revenue channel.
