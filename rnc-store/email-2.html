Seeds, caps, and laetrile: what is the difference?
Apricot seeds are the kernels inside apricot pits. One raw bitter apricot seed contains about 20 mg of B17, and one Seed Cap, made from ground apricot seeds with the oil removed, contains 50 mg.
Laetrile capsules are different again: purified B17 at 98.6 percent amygdalin purity, in 100 mg and 500 mg options. Bitter seeds test higher, which is why RNC tests its raw seeds for bitterness.
